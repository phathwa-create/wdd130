Eswatini

Eswatini (used to be called Swaziland) is a small country in Southern Africa that has no ocean coast, sitting between South Africa and Mozambique. It is ruled by a king, King Mswati III, alongside the Queen Mother, and has two capital cities: Mbabane for government offices and Lobamba for royal and traditional events. Even though the country is small, its land changes from cool, grassy mountains in the west to hot, flat plains in the east. Most people there are ethnic Swazi, speak siSwati and English, and are famous for keeping their traditional culture alive through big, colorful festivals like the *Umhlanga* (Reed Dance). Today, the country makes most of its money from farming (especially sugar and trees), factories, and tourism.
